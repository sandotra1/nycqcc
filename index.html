About the Event

Chess meets quantum computing

The NYC Quantum Chess Challenge uses Quantum Chess — a variant of the game that incorporates real quantum mechanics — to introduce middle school students to the world of quantum computing.

♟️

What is Quantum Chess?

A variant of classical chess where pieces can exist in superposition, become entangled, and behave according to the laws of quantum mechanics — making every move a new kind of puzzle.

⚛️

What will students learn?

Through gameplay, students will build an intuitive understanding of superposition, entanglement, and measurement — the same ideas that power real quantum computers.

🚀

Why quantum computing?

Quantum computing is one of the fastest-growing fields in technology. This event is designed to spark curiosity and give NYC middle schoolers a head start in exploring this exciting field.

🌍

Who is behind this?

Organized in collaboration with the United Nations ITU and IBM Quantum as part of Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 — a global series of community-led quantum computing events.

How it Works

From first move to final tournament

The NYC Quantum Chess Challenge takes place in three phases — from online training sessions to an in-person tournament at NYU.

01
October 2026

Online Training Sessions

Students join virtual sessions to learn Quantum Chess and explore foundational quantum concepts, supported by university student mentors.

02
October 2026

Practice & Preparation

Students practice with peers and mentors through drop-in online sessions, building their skills ahead of the final tournament.

03
November 2026

In-Person Tournament at NYU

Students compete in the final Quantum Chess tournament at New York University, with guest speakers, prizes, and a celebration of their achievements.

FAQ

Common questions

Does my child need to know how to play chess?

No — the training sessions will cover everything from the basics of chess to the quantum rules. All levels are welcome.

Is there a cost to participate?

No, the NYC Quantum Chess Challenge is completely free to participate in.

What does my child need for the online sessions?

A computer or tablet with a stable internet connection. We'll send all login details after registration.

Where is the in-person tournament held?

The final tournament will be held at New York University in Manhattan. Exact details will be shared with registered participants closer to the date.

Can university students participate as competitors?

The competition is designed for middle school students (grades 6–8). University students are encouraged to get involved as volunteers and mentors instead.

Who is organizing this event?

The NYC Quantum Chess Challenge is organized by Sara Andotra (NYU Physics, IBM Qiskit Advocate) in collaboration with the United Nations ITU and IBM Quantum, as part of Qiskit Fall Fest 2026.
